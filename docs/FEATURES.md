# Netrics — Features

What the app can do today, what is being built now, and what is planned next. This page tracks features only — no technical detail.

Last updated: 2026-11-14

## Available now

- **Roster view** — See all 15 players grouped as starters, bench, and reserve. Sort by highest single game, middle game (median), or most recent game.
- **Player detail** — For any player: a summary of their season so far, a game-by-game list showing where each game's points came from, and badges marking their three best games.
- **Target Score Explorer** — Pick a target score and see how often the player has beaten it, game by game.
- **Box plot and time windows** — A five-number summary (lowest, first quarter, middle, third quarter, highest) of a player's game scores, with a selector for the last week, the last 3 weeks, the last month, or the whole season.
- **Lock Advisor** — For each starter with a banked score, a recommendation to lock that score in or ride the next game, with the expected-value math shown.
- **Week Matchup** — Your week total against your opponent's, with live scores from Sleeper. A label shows whether the scores are live or from the last saved snapshot.
- **Bench Swing** — See how much the week total would change by swapping a bench player in for a starter.
- **Data coverage** — Game logs cover every regular-season game through Nov 6, 2026 for all 15 roster players. New games are added as they are played.
- **On-demand game loading & score check** — Completed game lines can be pulled in on demand and cross-checked against Sleeper's live points instead of being typed in by hand.
- **Scheduled automatic game loading** — A nightly workflow checks for newly finished games, cross-checks their points against Sleeper, and opens a pull request with the new game logs ready to review.
- **Roster & week sync check** — Checks whether your live Sleeper roster, lineup slots, or week number have changed compared to the saved app data.
- **Automatic roster & week sync** — Automatically applies Sleeper roster changes, lineup slot moves, and new-week updates into pull-request-gated data updates, fetching remaining schedules and player IDs for new additions while keeping lock records untouched.
- **Multi-day game backfill** — Backfills missed game logs across custom date ranges or catches up automatically from the most recent saved game.
- **Multi-view filters** — Search, position, and status filters on the Roster view; All, Needs Decision, and Locked filters on Lock Advisor; a toggle to hide locked-starter swaps on Bench Swing; and a Spikes-only toggle on Player Detail.
- **Mobile layout** — Responsive layout with wrapping filter and target rows, larger touch targets, two-column summary cards on narrow screens, sticky first table columns, and mobile-readable box plots.
- **Export & share views** — Shareable direct links for each view and player, one-click summary copy to clipboard, CSV file downloads, and print-ready formatting.
